# Use tool-call count for activity-group accordions

Activity groups start collapsed, including during execution, and preserve manual expansion through growth and completion while the group exists; ordinary tool failure or cancellation does not toggle their fold state, independently of the [enclosing work history](0015-final-response-outside-work-history.md). Active groups and groups containing unfinished activities keep their outer accordion; in summary state, one finished call appears directly with its native detail controls, while multiple calls retain the accordion. [Consecutive top-level calls still group across assistant messages](0013-transcript-activity-order-and-inline-thoughts.md), and each call counts once regardless of files, output sections, or streaming updates to preserve [tool-owned presentation](0019-tool-renderers-own-tool-presentation.md).
