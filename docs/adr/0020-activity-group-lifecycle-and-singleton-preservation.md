# Retain an accordion for every activity group

Every activity group keeps its outer accordion, including a settled group with one finished call; [consecutive top-level calls still group across assistant messages](0013-transcript-activity-order-and-inline-thoughts.md), and each call counts once regardless of files, output sections, or streaming updates. Groups start collapsed, preserve manual expansion through growth and completion, and keep that choice through ordinary failure or cancellation, independently of the [enclosing work history](0015-final-response-outside-work-history.md). Expansion chevrons remain visible while focused, with a separate focus marker, and each call retains [tool-owned presentation](0019-tool-renderers-own-tool-presentation.md) within the group.
