# Retain a disclosure for every render group

Every render group keeps its outer disclosure, including a settled group with one finished call; [consecutive top-level calls still group across assistant messages](0013-transcript-activity-order-and-inline-thoughts.md), and each call counts once regardless of files, output sections, or streaming updates. Groups start collapsed, preserve manual expansion through growth and completion, and keep that choice through ordinary failure or cancellation, independently of the [enclosing agent activity](0015-final-response-outside-agent-activity.md). Headers use the larger `▶` when collapsed and `▼` when expanded at the normal terminal font size and line height. Focused headers use Pi's native selection background, with expansion chevrons still visible and no extra arrow, and each call retains [tool-owned presentation](0019-tool-renderers-own-tool-presentation.md) within the group.
